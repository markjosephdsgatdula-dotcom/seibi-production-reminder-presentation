# Seisan Rimaindo Kinou — Supiichi Genkou (Roomaji)

## 🎤 Oopuningu

Minasama, ohayou gozaimasu. Yousetsu rain no Maaku desu.

Zenkai wa Seibi apuri zentai ni tsuite gohoukoku shimashita ga, honjitsu wa sonogo ni tsuika shita shinkinou "Seisan Rimaindo" ni tsuite gohoukoku itashimasu. Kore wa, yousetsu rain no Nukina-san kara jissai ni itadaita goyoubou wo moto ni kaihatsu shita mono desu.

---

## 1. Haikei to Kadai

Watashitachi wa hibi, Furekushe no seisan sukejuuru CSV wo minagara, hinshitsu kaizen no tame otte iru koutei ga itsu nagareru ka wo kakunin shite imasu.

Tada, kore ni wa mittsu no kadai ga arimashita:

1. **Mainichi CSV wo mokushi kakunin**: Hinshitsu kaizen de otte iru koutei wo minogasanai tame ni, tantousha ga mainichi jibun de fairu wo hiraite kakunin suru hitsuyou ga arimashita.
2. **Tsuuchi sareru shikumi ga nai**: Koutei ga chikazuite mo, dare mo oshiete kureru shikumi ga arimasen deshita.
3. **Zenkai itsu nagareta ka wakaranai**: Kako ni sono koutei ga itsu nagareta ka, kiroku wo sakanoboru shudan ga arimasen deshita.

---

## 2. Kaiketsusaku to Kinou

Nukina-san no goyoubou wo moto ni, Seibi apuri ni yottsu no kinou wo tsuika shimashita.

1. **Koutei wo "wotchi" shite san pataan de jidou tsuuchi**
   Hinshitsu kaizen de oitai koutei koodo wo touroku suru dake de, "shinki oodaa kenchi" "mikka mae" "toujitsu" no mittsu no taimingu de, iPad e pusshu tsuuchi ga todokimasu.

2. **Kensaku, SHOP-betsu karendaa nado yottsu no ichiran gamen**
   Wotchi ichiran, hinban/koutei koodo kensaku, SHOP-betsu karendaa, torihikisaki kensaku no yon gamen wo youi shimashita. Kensaku de wa oya-hinban → ko-hinban → koutei koodo no jun de hyouji suru node, genba no kankaku ni awasete sagasemasu.

3. **LINE WORKS e no riarutaimu tsuuchi purasu karendaa tsuika botan**
   Wotchi shite iru koutei ga shinki ni kenchi sareta toki, mikka mae, toujitsu no taimingu de, LINE WORKS ni mo jidou de tsuuchi ga todokimasu. Messeeji ni wa "karendaa ni tsuika" botan ga tsuite ori, tappu suru dake de sono yotei wo jibun no karendaa apuri ni tsuika dekimasu.

4. **Tanmatsu hyoujun karendaa e no kouoku taiou**
   Tookun-tsuki no URL wo ichido touroku suru dake de, iPad hyoujun no karendaa apuri ni mo yotei ga hanei saremasu.

---

## 3. Jissai no Seika

Kore wa tsukue no ue no setsumei dake de wa naku, jissai ni honban kankyou de douza wo kakunin-zumi desu.

- Nukina-san ga jissai ni wotchi shite iru koutei de, pusshu tsuuchi ga jikki no iPad ni todoku koto wo kakunin shimashita.
- LINE WORKS e no tsuuchi mo, jissai ni haishin sareta koto wo kakunin shite imasu. "Karendaa ni tsuika" botan mo jissai ni douza shi, jikki no karendaa apuri ni yotei ga hanei sareru koto made kakunin-zumi desu.

Genba kara no fiidobakku wo moto ni, SHOP ni yoru shiborikomi no han-i ya hyouji naiyou mo, jissai no shiyoukan ni awasete chousei wo kasanete imasu.

---

## 🎤 Kuroojingu

"Seisan Rimaindo" wa, genba no hitori kara no koe wo kikkake ni umareta kinou desu.

Chiisana youbou demo, jissai ni tsukau hito no koe wo moto ni subayaku katachi ni shi, genba de hontou ni yakudatsu kinou e to sodatete iku — sono saikuru wo, korekara mo tsuzukete ikimasu.

Go-seichou arigatou gozaimashita.
